## What this means, stated plainly

The pipeline over-includes records where "cult" is a metaphor (veneration, fashion,
film) or where a criminal organisation is not a high-control group, and it
under-includes a small number of legal analyses that sit at the cult/crime
intersection. Neither direction is large enough to overturn the reporting profile —
a metaphoric record tends to report none of the twelve constructs, so it inflates the
denominator and depresses the percentages — but both are large enough that the
reported percentages should be read as conservative, and both are the reason the
author adjudication is not optional.
