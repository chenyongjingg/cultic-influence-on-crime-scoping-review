# Scope statement (submission form)

Journal: *Frontiers in Psychology* — Specialty: Forensic and Legal Psychology
Article type: Systematic Review — Research Topic #76452

---

This scoping review sits within Forensic and Legal Psychology: it maps how the
psychological literature on manipulation in high-control groups — coercive control,
thought reform, indoctrination, information control — is connected to criminal
conduct and to judicial outcomes such as charge, conviction and sentence. It
measures what empirical, forensic and legal records actually report, identifies
where the published evidence cannot support the inferences courts and clinicians are
asked to draw, and proposes a ten-item minimum reporting set for that interface. It
is a PRISMA-ScR review of 163 records from six bibliographic sources, with no
experimental or clinical intervention, and it speaks directly to the Research
Topic's concern with group dynamics and their forensic implications.
